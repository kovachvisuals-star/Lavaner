## Useful content articles (do not invent prices)
1. **Katuse remondi hind: millest kujuneb tööde maksumus?** Scope, material choice, roof geometry, access, condition, timeline; CTA to /katustood.
2. **Fassaadi soojustamine: mida enne hinnapakkumist teada?** Wall condition, insulation design, finishing, scaffolding, project documentation; CTA to /fassaaditood.
3. **Maja renoveerimine: tööde järjekord ja eelarve planeerimine.** Inspection, scope, sequence, hidden defects, contingency; CTA to /maja-renoveerimine.
